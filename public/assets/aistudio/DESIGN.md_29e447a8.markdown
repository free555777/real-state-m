## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, high-velocity Indian real estate discovery and transaction platform. The core personality is high-trust, tech-forward, optimistic, and radically transparent. It serves dynamic urban renters, first-time homebuyers, commercial investors, and property agents navigating major financial life milestones.

The design movement mixes **Corporate / Modern** precision with energetic **Neo-Consumer Vibrant** cues:
- Clean white, expansive layouts maintain institutional credibility and clarity over dense transactional data.
- Saturated violet structural anchors radiate progressive digital power, offset by sharp mint/teal verification accents and high-visibility coral alerts.
- Tactile, rounded micro-interactions and soft physical depth transform intimidating paperwork, floor plans, and financial agreements into effortless, inviting digital flows.

## Colors

The palette balances Housing's iconic high-voltage energy with crisp, clean utility:

- **Primary (`#5e23dc`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary CTAs, active navigation items, key interactive states, branded highlights, and foundational structure.
- **Secondary (`#00bda6`):** The trust and growth accent. Governs verified property badges, price tags, positive returns/ROI indicators, and completion states.
- **Tertiary (`#f43f5e`):** The urgency and priority cue. Reserved for "Featured", "Exclusive", flash deals, urgent listing tags, and live alerts.
- **Neutral (`#191919`):** A deep charcoal providing optimal high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure black, supported by neutral secondary `#434343` and text-muted `#6b7280`.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base layer sits on `surface-canvas` (`#f8f9fd`) with cards elevated in crisp `surface-card` (`#ffffff`) and grouped sections tinted with `violet-tint` (`#f3f0fc`) and `surface-subtle` (`#f2f3f8`).

## Typography

The typographic expression relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, delivering geometric precision combined with open counters and welcoming humanist terminals. It projects clarity in dense multi-metric price breakdowns (Crores, Lakhs, EMI schedules) while maintaining approachable modernity in conversational consumer touchpoints.

- **Headlines:** Use heavy weights (700, 800) with slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for strong authority in landing pages, hero search titles, and locality headings.
- **Data & Numbers:** Property values, carpet areas, and rates/sq.ft must pair with semi-bold to bold weights to preserve immediate scannability across list feeds.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Labels apply upper-casing or strict title cases with subtle positive letter spacing to keep micro tags (e.g., "RERA REGISTERED", "READY TO MOVE") legible at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an **8pt rhythmic fluid grid**:

- **Grid Model:** 12 columns on desktop (`>= 1200px`), 8 columns on tablet (`768px - 1199px`), and 4 columns on mobile (`< 768px`). Max width for central content container is `1280px`.
- **Search & Filter Density:** Search results employ a 2-column or 3-column card presentation on desktop, and a single vertical card stack on mobile. Split-screen desktop search balances a 58% card scroll viewport with a 42% sticky map panel.
- **Gutters & Section Padding:** Desktop grid utilizes `1.5rem` gutters, scaling down to `1rem` on mobile. Inter-section spacing defaults to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on mobile and scales to `4rem` across major home page tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **ambient shadow** strategy tinted subtly with violet undertones, moving away from muddy industrial grays to evoke modern crispness:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base on `#f8f9fd` with no shadows.
- **Surface Level 1 (Listing Cards, Form Panels):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(94, 35, 220, 0.04)`. Outlined with a continuous `1px solid #e5e7eb` boundary.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards):** `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(94, 35, 220, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04)`. Cards physically lift with a `-2px` transform on desktop cursor hover.
- **Surface Level 3 (Search Bar Hubs, Modals, Overlays):** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(94, 35, 220, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.06)`. Used for global floating hero search modules and dialog modals.
- **Translucency & Frosted Glass:** Fixed navigation headers use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `border-b border-subtle` separator.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`level 2`)** geometry to balance warm consumer friendliness with structural corporate discipline:

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested micro-cards, image thumbnails, badges, and list action buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard for primary property listing cards, locality overview panels, and interactive filters.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for hero search containers, bottom sheets on mobile, and floating promo banners.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Strictly deployed for standalone search chips, pill filters, verified check badges, and primary action buttons within cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#5e23dc`, text `#ffffff`, font weight `600`, pill-shaped or `rounded-base` depending on size. On hover: shifts to `#4d1cb8` with a soft purple shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(94, 35, 220, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Teal CTA / Contact Developer):** Background `#00bda6`, text `#ffffff`. Used for positive conversions ("Contact Agent", "Book Site Visit").
- **Outline / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #5e23dc`, transparent fill, text `#5e23dc`. On hover: `violet-tint` (`#f3f0fc`) background.

### Cards (Property Listings)
- Clean white background (`#ffffff`), border `1px solid #e5e7eb`, border radius `1rem`.
- Split imagery container with 16:10 aspect ratio, housing top-left pill chips (e.g., `#f43f5e` for "Exclusive", `#10b981` for "RERA Verified").
- Content zone structures pricing in bold `headline-md` format, followed by configuration, locality text in muted neutral, and agent/seller contact actions pinned to the bottom.

### Search Bar & Filters
- High-visibility elevated container with segment selector pills ("Buy", "Rent", "Commercial", "Plots") anchored at top.
- Input groupings divided by vertical hairline dividers (`#e5e7eb`), culminating in a prominent primary violet search button.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Inactive: Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e5e7eb`, text `#434343`, fully pill-shaped.
- Active: Background `#f3f0fc`, border `1.5px solid #5e23dc`, text `#5e23dc` with bold weight.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height 48px, radius `0.5rem`, border `1px solid #e5e7eb`, background `#ffffff`.
- Focus state: Border color `#5e23dc`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(94, 35, 220, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkboxes: 20px box, `0.25rem` radius. When selected, solid `#5e23dc` fill with white SVG checkmark.
- Radio: Circular, active ring `#5e23dc` surrounding a 6px inner dot.

### Specialized PropTech Components
- **EMI & Affordability Sliders:** Slider rail in `#e5e7eb`, filled progress track in `#5e23dc`, with a 24px thumb casting a tactile shadow.
- **Floor Plan Viewer:** High-contrast technical backdrop with secondary `#00bda6` dimensions and zoom float controls.
- **Verified Trust Badge:** Emerald green `#10b981` micro pill with shield icon and white text.
